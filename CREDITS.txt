Images considered but not used
------------------------------
- Unsplash and Pixabay candidate pages could not be retrieved from this
  environment (bot-challenge / access blocked), so those libraries were
  not used.
- Several otherwise useful Wikimedia photos were skipped because they
  were licensed CC BY-SA (ShareAlike is not used on this site), showed
  clearly identifiable faces in close-up, showed competitor or rental-
  company logos on trucks, or were not U.S. highway work (for example
  European arrow trucks or overhead permanent message signs).
- A standalone trailer-mounted arrow board (as opposed to an arrow board
  mounted on a TMA / work truck) was not found in Public Domain or CC BY
  collections. The arrow-board card uses a U.S. work-zone truck with a
  clear lighted chevron arrow board.

Web export
----------
All images used on the site were resized to a maximum width of 1600
pixels and saved as progressive JPEG at about quality 80–82.
